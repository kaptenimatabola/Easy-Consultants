# Easy Consultants — 50-Prospect Launch Campaign

## Objective
Convert the first 50 Lesotho prospects into qualified conversations, proposals, paid projects and retainers.

## Pipeline
Prospect → Contacted → Replied → Qualified → Discovery → Proposal → Won/Lost → Onboarding → Delivery → Retainer

## Priority
- 15 high-priority organizations for first-wave research and personalized outreach.
- 35 additional prospects form the second wave.
- Do not mass-send generic messages. Personalize around a visible business problem or growth opportunity.

## First-wave offer
**Business Digital Audit**
A focused review of a company's digital presence, customer journey, branding, automation opportunities and growth systems, followed by a practical action plan.

## Outreach sequence
1. Day 1 — concise introduction and one specific observation.
2. Day 3 — useful audit insight or improvement opportunity.
3. Day 6 — concrete service offer with a clear next step.
4. Day 10 — final first-cycle follow-up.
5. Day 21 — reactivation with a new insight or relevant offer.

## Qualification fields
- Company
- Decision maker
- Role
- Contact channel
- Core problem
- Current digital maturity
- Urgency
- Budget signal
- Service fit
- Next action
- Next action date

## CEO rules
- Revenue conversations outrank cosmetic website work.
- Evidence before assumptions.
- Never fabricate company problems or claims.
- Keep prospect data accurate and traceable to its source.
- Use governed CRM access; do not expose private prospect data publicly.
- Every qualified opportunity must have a next action.
