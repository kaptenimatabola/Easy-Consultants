# Easy Consultants — International Automated Operating System

## Revenue engine
Lead → Qualification → Discovery → Proposal → Payment → Onboarding → Delivery → Reporting → Retainer.

## Systems
- GitHub: source control and product delivery
- Supabase: CRM, client, project and automation data
- Vercel: web application hosting/deployment
- Canva: brand and campaign production
- Stripe: project checkout, invoices and recurring retainers
- Apollo: international B2B prospecting and enrichment
- Notion: SOPs, knowledge base, proposals, project playbooks

## Initial service architecture
1. Strategy & Consulting
2. Branding & Marketing
3. Websites & Software
4. AI & Automation
5. Sales & CRM Systems
6. Digital Transformation

## Automation principles
- Capture every lead.
- Assign a source and service interest.
- Qualify before manual sales effort.
- Trigger follow-up tasks automatically.
- Generate proposal/payment workflow from qualified opportunities.
- Create client/project records after payment.
- Track delivery, satisfaction and renewal opportunities.
- Keep secrets out of GitHub; use environment variables.

## International positioning
Serve clients remotely across Africa and international markets, while maintaining Lesotho as the operating base.
