# Easy Consultants — Revenue Launch Playbook

## Positioning
Easy Consultants is an international consulting, technology, automation and growth partner helping ambitious businesses turn ideas into professional brands, digital products and revenue systems.

## Primary offers
1. **Business Digital Audit** — diagnose brand, website, sales, operations and automation gaps; deliver an action roadmap.
2. **Professional Website Package** — strategy, copy, responsive website, enquiry capture and launch support.
3. **Brand & Marketing Package** — brand positioning, visual direction, social assets, campaign structure and lead-generation setup.
4. **AI Automation Package** — automate repetitive enquiries, qualification, reporting and operational workflows.
5. **Digital Transformation Package** — integrated strategy, software, automation, sales systems and implementation.

## Sales pipeline
Prospect → Enquiry → Qualification → Discovery → Proposal → Payment → Onboarding → Delivery → Retainer

## Lead qualification
Prioritize prospects with:
- A real operating business
- A visible digital/marketing problem
- Decision-maker access
- A clear commercial objective
- Budget or a defined near-term project

## First-market execution
Target 50 Lesotho businesses first, then expand regionally and internationally.

### Outreach sequence
Day 1 — personalized introduction
Day 3 — useful observation/audit point
Day 6 — concrete offer
Day 10 — final follow-up
Day 21 — reactivation

## Core sales message
Lead with the business problem, not the technology. Diagnose first. Show the cost of leaving the problem unresolved. Present one practical solution. Ask for the discovery call.

## Delivery standard
Every paid project receives:
- Written scope
- Timeline
- Client information requirements
- Progress checkpoints
- Acceptance criteria
- Handover
- Post-delivery support/retainer option
