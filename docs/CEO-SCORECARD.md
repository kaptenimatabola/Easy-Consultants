# Easy Consultants — CEO Scorecard

## North-star objective

Build a profitable, repeatable and increasingly recurring international business.

## Weekly scoreboard

### Acquisition
- New prospects added
- Personalized outreaches sent
- Replies
- Discovery calls booked

### Sales
- Qualified opportunities
- Proposals sent
- Proposal value
- Closed-won revenue
- Pipeline value

### Delivery
- Active projects
- On-time delivery %
- Client issues
- Client satisfaction
- Expansion opportunities

### Recurring revenue
- Active retainers
- Monthly recurring revenue
- Churn
- Upsells

### Product
- Repeatable workflows identified
- Automation modules created
- Product experiments
- Active product users

### Finance
- Cash collected
- Accounts receivable
- Operating expenses
- Gross margin
- Cash runway

## CEO decision rules

1. Revenue-generating work outranks cosmetic work.
2. Customer evidence outranks assumptions.
3. Repeatable problems should become products.
4. Recurring revenue outranks repeated one-off effort when economics support it.
5. Security and trust are infrastructure, not marketing.
6. Never fake traction.
7. Never purchase infrastructure merely because it looks impressive.
8. Every new initiative needs an owner, customer, metric and next milestone.

## Current 30-day target

**Primary:** acquire the first cohort of paying Easy Consultants clients.

**Secondary:** turn the most repeated customer problem into a standardized service and then a product candidate.

**Do not optimize for vanity metrics.**
